# Description
Found in [[Aiskhel]] and [[Svalgheim]], the Seeds of Eidelon are spiritual fruits that stave off the Exhaustive effects from both realms. Each planal fruit bears *1d4* seeds.
# Consumption
The target can choose whether to restore a Level of Exhaustion, or 1 spell slot of any level.

In addition, the seeds bear restorative healing properties. Each seed heals *1d6 HP*.

# Description
*Requires attunement*
A blade the party retrieved from a strange Goblin's forge in the depths of the Aurelian Citadel. When a creature is killed by the sword, A mechanism protrudes from the hilt that, when activated, discharges newly-minted souls.
# Soul Surge
Mechanically similar to a [Longsword](https://5e.tools/items.html#longsword_xphb), this blade can store a maximum of **6 souls**.

The user must declare *Soul Surge* before an attack roll to deal out 1 Critical Hit additional damage. This effect can stack.

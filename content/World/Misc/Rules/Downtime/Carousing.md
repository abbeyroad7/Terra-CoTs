## Introduction
During [[Downtime]], players can choose to spend their time carousing around their campsite, in the local area, or even across other dimensions.

The following activities fall under the Carousing category:
- Gambling
- Gossip
- Labor
- Perform
- Reconnaissance
- Socialize

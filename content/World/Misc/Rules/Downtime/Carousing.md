## Introduction
During [[Downtime]], players can choose to spend their time carousing around their campsite, in the local area, or even across other dimensions (if they possess the means).

The following activities fall under the Carousing category:
- Gambling
- Gossip
- Labor
- Performing
- Reconnaissance
- Socializing
### Gameplay
When performing any of the above activities in character, you may be presented with unexpected events and decisions that lead your party astray, for better or worse.

Be aware, if your character is ever alone at any time, they may run into trouble and will need to handle it on their own.

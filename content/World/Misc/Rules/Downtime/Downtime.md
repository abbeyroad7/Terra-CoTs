## Introduction
During a short or long rest, players can choose to spend their downtime on various activities to pass the time, outlined below.
## Activities
- Woodworking
- Metal Smithing
- Creative outlets, writing, storytelling, music
- Potion Making/Scrollmaking
- Carousing - Socializing with area NPCs
- Keeping watch
- Praying to deities
- Personal grooming, hygiene upkeep (+1 charisma)
- Atunement

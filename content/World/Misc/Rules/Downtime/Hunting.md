## Introduction
Players can extract animal parts to use for crafting weapons, recipes, or for clothing and armor.

For example, the rarity of certain meats and vegetables could prove to make certain dishes into delicacies for characters. Though some ingredients would best remain off the kitchen table...
## Gameplay
Roll a Survival check to hunt for wild game in the area and if successful, roll a Nature check to extract animal parts from the hunt.

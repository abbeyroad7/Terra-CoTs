## Introduction
As an alternative to light activity during downtime, your character can choose to rigourously train and gain improvements to certain physical skills.
### Gameplay
As training requires more strenuous activity than other light activities, choosing to do this during a Long/Short rest would negate any natural healing gained during this time. 

You would instead dedicate 1-2 hours of your long or short rest to training.

**Roll a DC20 for Strength or Constitution to decide if training causes injury.**

**For every hour of training, gain 30 XP * Level.**
#### Roleplay
You will need to explain the activity you are doing that centers around a certain skill.
